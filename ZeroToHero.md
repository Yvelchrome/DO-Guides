If you're looking for a more concise, "at a glance" guide, you can find one [here](https://odyssey-calc.com/#/guidebook).

To preface this guide, I'd like to point out that it reflects my personal opinions and experience. If you think something is out of place or have any suggestions, feel free to message me on Discord: @yvelchrome.

This guide recommends what I believe is the best approach and progression order to ensure the most efficient and optimal experience.

> The guide may be slightly outdated after a new patch until I collect the new information to update it.

> I will do my best to avoid relying on temporary events or limited-time content so this guide remains useful over time. While special events may occasionally provide faster ways to progress, this guide focuses on the permanent progression available.

## Character Creation

Tamer choice does not really matter, even in the current endgame. Pick whichever you like, as you can change fairly easily later.

One of the fastest combination for clearing the main story/quest (MSQ) is **Ryo Akiyama + **[[digimon:dfwwn6s|Agumon(Classic)]].

**Ryo Akiyama's Passive Skill** increases Vaccine Digimon AT by 5% and his **Active Skill** increases Skill Damage by 60% and AT by 100% for 3 minutes.

[[digimon:dfwwn6s|Agumon(Classic)]]'s entire digivolution line consists of damage dealers and has attribute advantage against most dungeon bosses encountered during the MQ.

> For the purpose of this guide, I will assume you chose [[digimon:dfwwn6s|Agumon(Classic)]].

> Don't worry about collecting every Digimon right away. On this server, they're easy to obtain, and you'll have plenty of opportunities to hatch any Digimon you want later.

#### Quick Disclaimer about Field Raid Bosses

Field Raid Bosses spawn on each Channel every 3 minutes and at the beginning of the fight get a **world boss protection** buff for 5 seconds and are immune to damage at that time, so use your skills after the buff expires.

In the early game they are pretty tanky so it is useful to buff yourself before using your skills. **Melee Class* buffs, which your [[digimon:dvq7beo|Greymon]] is, are:
- **F10: Digital Hazard** (Increased Attack Speed by 15%, Attack by 30% and Critical Damage by 50% for 15 seconds.)
- **F6: Berserker Soul** (Increases Attack Speed by 12%, Critical Damage by 20% and Attack by 20% for 15 seconds.)
- **F5: Rex Fury** (Increases Attack Power by x (+60 per Skill level) for 20 seconds.)
- **Tamer Skill: Suppression** (Increases skill damage by 60% and Attack by 100% for 10 seconds.)

**F4: Mega Flame** is your highest burst skill and should always be used buffed if possible.

> You can check their raid timer via the yellow **clock icon** under the minimap. In there, you can also view their respective rewards. Those Field Raid Bosses are important later.

### General guidance

#### Early Leveling

Before doing any sort of farming, and until you're at mid/end game, level-up your digimon to level 90 by farming **DarkTyrannomon Story Mode**.
Also think about leveling your skill points on the desired digimon, all skills should be maxed (currently 10/10 maximum for ultimate and above forms)

> Don't forget to use any sort of [[item:i1lirtkn|Amplification Booster+1000%]] to make the process much faster but remember, they are limited!

> If you're starting from Lv1, killing digimons in the Tokyo-Odaiba maps or clearing easier Story Mode dungeons until you unlock Champion or all Rookie skills will make leveling in dungeons easier.

[[dungeon:uzyxsi2|The Dark Roar|Story]]

> The Dungeon is located in **Tokyo-Odaiba: BigSight**

---

#### Digimon Size

Increase your Digimon's size to gain additional base stats.

Aim for **127%+**, as it's relatively easy to achieve and provides most of the benefit.

> You can do so by using [[item:ilwwdfb|Fruit of the Goddess]]. A 5/5 Digimon can reach a maximum size of **130%**.

> Don't chase **130%** unless you already have plenty of resources. The extra stats are minimal, and later in the game you'll upgrade your Digimon to **6/6** through Transcendence, increasing the maximum size to **140%**. At that point, you'll need to resize your Digimon anyway.

[Digimon Size](https://docs.thedigitalodyssey.com/s/1ea1524b-2c7e-4369-9608-36acb1ef87b0/doc/digital-odysseys-zero-to-hero-guide-e3BWNVwpye#h-digimon-size)

---

#### Clones

From this point onward, every digimon you actively use should have:

- AT cloned to 15.
- CT cloned enough to always crit (generally 6–8 clones, ~120% in the form you're actually playing with).
- BL cloned to 15 if your digimon is a Tank.

> At this stage, you can obtain clones reliably by:
> - Buying them from the market if you want to skip the next suggestions.
> - **BEST** Farming Field Raid bosses (preferably the **File Island** ones because you have **0-7 Channels** there)
> - Farming [MetalSeadramon Dungeon](https://odyssey-calc.com#/guides/dungeon-boss-locations?section=metalseadramon-mdg) (MDG) if somehow you already have a **good** cloned mega/BM VI digimon.
> - Farming [Server Continent Canyon](https://odyssey-calc.com#/guides/dungeon-boss-locations?section=server-continent-canyon-scc) (SCC) Story Mode solo with a strong AoE DPS (such as [[digimon:dzkw288|VictoryGreymon]]).
> - Farming SCC Normal Mode in a party as it does give 2 times more clones than its Story Mode or MDG.

[Cloning System](https://docs.thedigitalodyssey.com/s/1ea1524b-2c7e-4369-9608-36acb1ef87b0/doc/digital-odysseys-zero-to-hero-guide-e3BWNVwpye#h-cloning-system)

### 100% Block Rate

You should ensure any digimon that you clone to BL15 gets to 100% Block with seals, even for DPS.

> Blocking is a 50% damage reduction on every basic attack (AA) from enemy digimon.
> It's ultimately useful for everyone as you will want your DPS cloned to BL15 anyway just in case the tanks die so you can mitigate the death and not wipe the run or in general fighting in the overworld.

You need to get 13% BL from seals, you can achieve this in a few ways:

- Farm [[item:ifr94vu|Digimon Seal Box [BL]] in [Apocalymon Story Mode](https://share.odyssey-calc.com/guides/dungeon-boss-locations?v=5&section=apocalymon).
- Get [[item:is6vrm4|Seal Master Package [Seal Exchange]], on market for example, and use the [[item:i1m8n4jz|Seal Exchange Ticket]] to get BL seals from Digitamamon in the Olympos map
- Farm/buy dungeon boss seals (MetalSeadramon seals in MDGH for example).
